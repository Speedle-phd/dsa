# Sonderfertigkeiten (Übersicht)

source: DSA5 Regelwerk, S. 51–55 (details: Talente S. 214–224, Kampf S. 225–250, Magie S. 251–304, Götterwirken S. 305–336)

## Summary

- A SF is bought once (or per level) for its AP-Wert, if the prerequisites are met; SF are not raised like skills (S. 51).
- Leveled SF (I–III) require the previous level.
- Muttersprache (Sprache III) and one Ortskenntnis are free via the Kultur (S. 41).

## Language and script acquisition (S. 219–224)

- Languages cost 2 AP per level. I/II/III indicate basic/fluent/native proficiency; an unlevelled legacy entry does not establish an acquisition prerequisite.
- Ogrisch and Oloarkh stop at II. Isdira/Asdharia III require Zweistimmiger Gesang. Rssahh III requires appropriate species approval.
- Scripts require a matching known language and exclude Blind. Core-book script costs: Altes Alaani, Angram and Chrmk 4 AP; Chuchas and Trollische Raumbilderschrift 6 AP; the other listed scripts 2 AP (S. 224).
- Native language and other free creation benefits must be recorded without AP, rather than purchased again.

## Implemented acquisition workflow

- Overview advantages/disadvantages/general SF and talent-tab languages/scripts share catalog previews, AP quotes, bilingual source-cited effects and confirmed undo.
- Disadvantages use catalog-only selection and distinguish character-creation AP credits from free during-play recording; see [advantages-disadvantages](../character/advantages-disadvantages.md).
- Prerequisites are validated server-side; narrative prerequisites require explicit GM confirmation. Only app-tracked payments can be refunded. Known dependent entries must be removed first.
- General/fate-point SF and the common magical/karmal SF, traditions and staff SF are catalogued. Spells/rituals/curses and familiar tricks have their own subsystems (see [../magic/spells-and-rituals.md](../magic/spells-and-rituals.md), [../creatures/companions.md](../creatures/companions.md)); liturgies and artifacts do not.
- Merkmalskenntnis and Aspektkenntnis use ordinal costs. Spell/liturgy counts, leading attribute, training and aspect eligibility need GM confirmation because those records are not represented by this sheet.
- Staff abilities require a bound staff; their volume (18) is checked on acquisition. Buying Bindung des Stabes books its 2 permanent AsP (S. 276). See [../magic/spells-and-rituals.md](../magic/spells-and-rituals.md), "Magie chapter update".
- Special abilities are grouped (`general`, `fate`, `magic`, `tradition`, `staff`, `witch`, `karma`): the Overview lists general, fate and karmal SF; magical ones are only in the Arcana tab.
- The shared check modifier dialog includes server-derived effects from owned general and combat SF. Terrain/location and other conditional bonuses require explicit selection; the description and printed page remain visible. Maneuver levels apply only to eligible techniques; one basic and one special maneuver may be selected, subject to specific exclusions (S. 246–250).
- Fertigkeitsspezialisierung adds +2 FW only for the selected application; it does not increase the three attribute targets. The temporary effective FW is displayed alongside the stored FW (S. 216). Rerolls and post-roll QS/food benefits remain reminders, not invented target bonuses.
- Meister der Improvisation offsets up to two points of the tool penalty chosen in the talent Probe's tools picker, crafting talents only (S. 218).
- **Eiserner Wille I–II** (S. 216): "Focus: Eiserner Wille" on the Overview SK meter starts an active effect (`ironWill`): SK +level (Overview, Combat), every other check −level (Selbstbeherrschung excluded, it keeps the focus). Ending it (strip ✕, or setting Bewusstlos) adds Betäubung +1 on the server.
- **Schmerzen unterdrücken** (S. 219): after a successful Selbstbeherrschung Probe with Schmerz ≥ 1, the result offers to suppress min(QS, Schmerz) levels (`painSuppression` effect, offset like Gulmond; lifts the Schmerz IV block when it drops below IV). "End fight" ends it; ending adds Betäubung +1. Endpoint `PUT /api/stats/ability-effect/:id` (`utils/abilityEffects.ts`).
- **Anführer** (S. 215): the Überreden result names the number of allies (QS+1); a hero who received the bonus toggles "Anführer" in the fight panel for that KR (AT, defense, FK +1, `fight.leaderRound`).
- **Falschspielen** (S. 216): after a failed Brett- & Glücksspiel check (not a Patzer) "Repeat the failed check" rolls again, binding. **Rosstäuscher** (S. 218): select it in the modifiers when selling livestock; "Roll again, keep the better".
- **Fischer / Jäger / Sammler** (S. 217–219): the result of Fischen & Angeln / Tierkunde / Pflanzenkunde shows the ration rule and "+1 QS of food" when owned.
- These effects are derived from ownership, not persisted separately or charged AP again. Selected effects update the effective target/FW boxes without rolling dice; see [skill checks](skill-checks.md). Liturgy casting and narrative unlocks remain manual. The fate-point SF "Attacke/Ausweichen/Eigenschaft/Fernkampf/Parade verbessern" are offered after the matching roll (S. 222, see [../character/fate-points.md](../character/fate-points.md)); Wachsamkeit is not (status Überrascht is not tracked).
- Conditional maneuver/stance selections are local to each check, not a synchronized combat-round state. Apply their restrictions to other actions manually. Penalty reductions require enough original situational penalty to cover their combined correction; the app does not infer whether that penalty came from tools or range.
