# Archiv der Kräuter: Kräutersuche (Phase 2)

source: Archiv der Kräuter (AdK), S. 261–266 (distribution table), S. 275–278 (Kräutersuche, Fokusregel I); DSA5 Regelwerk, S. 344

## Summary

- Three steps (AdK S. 276):
  1. landscape type
  2. Pflanzenkunde
  3. Sinnesschärfe (Suchen)
- Two kinds:
  - **specific**: one plant; the check is modified by the plant's Suchschwierigkeit
  - **general**: anything useful; the check is −1, and each species is found with its QS-1 applications
- Pflanzenkunde modifiers (S. 277):
  - landscape:
    - Hoher Norden −4
    - Grasländer, Heiden und Steppen ±0
    - Sümpfe, Marschen und Moore −1
    - Wälder +1
    - Regenwälder ±0
    - Gebirge −1
    - Wüstenrandgebiete und Wüsten −4
    - Maraskan −2
  - weather: Regen/Schnee −1, Sturm −2, Orkan −3
- A failed Pflanzenkunde check finds nothing that day (S. 276). A failed Sinnesschärfe check also finds nothing, but tomorrow the hero knows where to look (S. 278).
- The QS are used either way (S. 277), the best value reached counts:
  - **verbessern**: time −2 / −4 / −6 hours at QS 1 / 3 / 5; Sinnesschärfe +1 / +2 / +3 at QS 2 / 4 / 6
  - **Ertrag**:
    - specific: applications by QS from the plant's table (S. 277 sidebar, RW S. 344)
    - general: as many species as QS
- Base time: 8 hours (specific), 10 hours (general). A shortened search costs Sinnesschärfe −1 per 2 hours saved; under 2 hours it has no effect (S. 278).
- A general search: the GM picks the species. The distribution table helps: frequency 5 gewöhnlich … 1 sehr selten per landscape, * = only under a condition (S. 261–266).
- Searching in a group: every hero searches on their own (S. 278).
- Book examples (tests): Bruder Hilbert (Atan-Kiefer, Gebirge, rain), Rowena (Reichsforst, general). Hilbert's example counts the Atan-Kiefer's Suchschwierigkeit as −2; its plant page (S. 15) prints −3, and the app uses the page.

## Decisions

- User decisions (Nov 2026):
  - with **verbessern** the yield is the minimum: the applications of QS 1 and 1 species (the book does not say)
  - in a party the **GM picks** the species of a general search; the app suggests them; a solo player picks from the same list
  - the rule is switched on per party or solo hero (`herbSearch`, needs Archiv der Kräuter)
- App choices, not user decisions:
  - The player rolls both checks in the app; the server recomputes time, modifiers and yield from the reported results (like all rolls in the app).
  - Found plants go to the inventory under "Sonstiges" as raw plants with the in-game day (`herb.obtainedDay`). The calendar is not advanced by the search hours; the GM advances the date.
  - **Suggestions**: plants with a value in that landscape, most common first. Within a frequency, plants with a * condition (e.g. "in Höhlen") come last, then by Suchschwierigkeit (the book's rule of thumb, S. 278). Plants not found in the wild (Malomis, Nemezijn) are left out.
  - **A specific search** offers only plants with a value in the chosen landscape. The dialog starts in the plant's most common landscape, and on a tie in the one with the best modifier.
  - **Table cells with two values** ("5 (Aranien), sonst 4", "2 (Wüste), 1 (Rand)") count with the first value.
  - **Corrected rows**: Finage and Orazal print only 7 of 8 values in the table; their rows come from the plant pages (S. 44, 88).
  - Herb finds are kept for a day (TTL), like GM check requests.
  - Kräutersuche in Regionen (Fokusregel II, S. 278–281) is not built; its switch stays disabled.
